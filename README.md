### 💳 Credit Risk Scoring & Default Prediction

**Predictive Analytics • Machine Learning • Credit Risk • Explainable AI**

End-to-end **CRISP-DM credit-risk project** developed to estimate the probability of a customer becoming **90+ days delinquent**.

The solution combines **LightGBM**, cost-sensitive threshold optimisation, asymmetric business costs, model evaluation, and **SHAP explainability**, with an interactive affordability decision-support application.

**Highlights:** `ROC-AUC 0.869` • `PR-AUC 0.409` • `Operational Threshold 60%` • `Affordability Analysis`

`Python` `Pandas` `Scikit-learn` `LightGBM` `XGBoost` `SHAP` `CRISP-DM`
